Our Services

We offer a variety of services to help you improve your Go skills and connect with other players.

1. Online Go Lessons

Experienced players conduct online Go lessons for players of all skill levels. Whether you are a beginner or an advanced player, we help you improve your game.

2. Go Tournaments

We host online Go tournaments for our members on OGS. These tournaments offer a great opportunity to compete against others, improve your rank, and have fun.

3. Community Events

We also organize online community events where members can meet, share experiences, and participate in various group activities.
