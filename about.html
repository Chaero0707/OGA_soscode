About OGA

OGA is a group of amateur Go players who enjoy playing and learning the game together.

We welcome players of all skill levels to join our community and improve their Go skills.

Feel free to reach out to us for any questions or inquiries about our group.

You can contact us through our social media channels or by sending a message to our admin.

Our admin is available to assist you with any questions or concerns you may have.

Admin Profile Link
One must pass a dedicated selection tournament to assume the role of a teacher, and students can challenge teachers to a match to receive instruction. While matches affecting rankings are not permitted, students may request friendly matches, and teachers are encouraged to accept such requests whenever possible. Additionally, the teacher selection tournament will be declared void if there is insufficient participation.
